## Evidence rule

Evidence is append-only, versioned, and contains exact commands, revision and
config checksums, test results, limitations, touched files, and rollback steps.
An unavailable device or user-supplied asset is a blocking `pending` result,
not a pass, failure waiver, or omitted row.
